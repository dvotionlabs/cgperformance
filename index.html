My approach

How the training process works

My approach begins with a Movement Strategy Analysis, where I identify the movement strategy a client is currently using and the movement availability they have.

The work starts from there, with training designed to make sure the client has sufficient movement availability for the tasks they want or need to perform, whether those tasks relate to everyday life, becoming stronger or faster, improving fitness or preparing for their sport.

Strength work is then implemented in a way that develops the required physical qualities without unnecessarily reducing or compromising movement capacity. This allows clients to become stronger, faster, fitter or better prepared for their sport while continuing to consider their health and movement ability.

  1. Stage 1

    Understand

    I begin by understanding:

    • Your goals
    • Your training history
    • Your day to day physical demands
    • Your sporting demands where relevant
    • Your weekly schedule
    • Your training availability
    • The equipment available to you

  2. Stage 2

    Analyse

    A Movement Strategy Analysis is used to identify:

    • The movement strategy you are currently using
    • The movement availability you currently have
    • How you organise movement during relevant tasks
    • The movement requirements of the activities or goals you want to pursue

  3. Stage 3

    Establish the starting point

    I compare the movement availability you currently have with the movement requirements of the tasks you want or need to perform. That comparison is used to select the appropriate starting point and training inputs.

  4. Stage 4

    Develop strength and capacity

    The physical qualities required for your goals are introduced and progressed. This may include:

    • Movement work
    • Strength work
    • Aerobic activity
    • Speed development
    • Sport preparation
    • General fitness

    Strength work is implemented in a way that develops the required qualities without unnecessarily reducing or compromising movement capacity.

  5. Stage 5

    Reassess and progress

    The programme is adjusted when required, using:

    • Training performance
    • Your feedback
    • Movement reassessment
    • Changes in goals
    • Changes in schedule
    • Changes in training availability

Cancellation and rescheduling

Individual sessions

You can cancel or reschedule a booked session when at least 24 hours' notice is provided. A replacement session remains subject to availability and must take place within the same calendar month for a monthly plan, or within the remaining validity period for a session pack.

Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full and cannot be rescheduled. The session is deducted from your monthly allowance or pack, is not refunded and cannot be transferred to another month or credited against a future purchase. The same applies to a missed session. If I cancel a session, you are not charged for it and the session is rearranged.

Monthly services

All monthly coaching services continue on a recurring basis. A minimum of one month's written notice is required to cancel. Cancellation requests are submitted to the CG Performance business email address, and I will confirm receipt, the final billing date, the final service date and any remaining sessions available before the service ends.

Payments falling due during the notice period remain payable. Cancelling the Direct Debit instruction through your bank does not itself cancel the coaching agreement or remove payments already due during the notice period.
