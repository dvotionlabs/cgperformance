My approach

How the training process works

My approach begins with a Movement Strategy Analysis, where I identify the movement strategy a client is currently using and the movement availability they have.

The work starts from there, with training designed to make sure the client has sufficient movement availability for the tasks they want or need to perform, whether those tasks relate to everyday life, becoming stronger or faster, improving fitness or preparing for their sport.

Strength work is then implemented in a way that develops the required physical qualities without unnecessarily reducing or compromising movement capacity. This allows clients to become stronger, faster, fitter or better prepared for their sport while continuing to consider their health and movement ability.

Frequently asked questions

Questions clients ask

Where does in person coaching take place?

In person coaching takes place by appointment at UNTIL Liverpool Street and UNTIL Marylebone.

How long is each session?

In Person Personal Training, Virtual Coaching and the Movement Strategy Analysis Consultation are 60 minutes.

What is your approach to training?

The process begins with a Movement Strategy Analysis to identify the movement strategy a client is currently using and the movement availability they have. Training then develops the movement and physical qualities required for their goals while implementing strength work in a way that does not unnecessarily reduce or compromise movement capacity.

Do unused monthly sessions roll over?

No. Monthly session allowances apply to the relevant calendar month. Unused sessions expire at the end of that month and do not roll over.

How long are session packs valid?

The 3 Session Pack is valid for one month from purchase. The 10 Session Pack is valid for three months from purchase.

What happens if I cancel a booked session?

A session can be rescheduled when at least 24 hours' notice is provided. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full and cannot be rescheduled.

When are monthly services billed?

The regular monthly billing date is the first of each month.

What happens if I join before the first?

Your complete monthly service begins on the next applicable first of the month. In person or Virtual Coaching sessions arranged before then are charged separately at the per session rate of your selected plan.

How much notice is required to cancel a monthly service?

A minimum of one month's written notice is required.

What is included in Online Coaching?

Online Coaching includes an individual programme based on your movement capacity, goals, schedule, training availability and available equipment. It may include movement work, strength work and aerobic activity, together with one monthly review call and programme updates.

What equipment do I need for Virtual Coaching?

You need a stable internet connection, a suitable camera device, a tripod or stable camera position, enough training space and access to basic training equipment.

Is the Movement Strategy Analysis a medical assessment?

No. It is a coaching and movement assessment service. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Can I enquire before purchasing?

Yes. The enquiry form can be used to discuss your goals, preferred service, location and availability before purchasing.
