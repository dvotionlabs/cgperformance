Privacy Policy

Draft. Last updated 1 August 2026.

Draft for review. This policy is a working draft prepared for the CG Performance website. It requires final business and legal review before launch.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy explains how CG Performance Limited, referred to below as CG Performance, handles personal information provided through cgperformance.fit and through the coaching services described on that website. CG Performance is the data controller for that information.

Questions about this policy can be sent to chrisgkoufas.performance@gmail.com.

2. Information collected through the enquiry form

When you send an enquiry through the website, the form collects:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number, if you choose to provide it
  • The service you are interested in
  • Your preferred location or delivery method
  • Your current goals
  • Your current training routine, if you choose to provide it
  • Your preferred training frequency
  • Your general availability, if you choose to provide it
  • Any message you write
  • Your confirmation that CG Performance may use this information to reply

The enquiry form is not intended for medical information or payment information. Please do not include either in the form.

3. Information collected through Stripe

Payments and Direct Debit instructions are handled by Stripe Payments Europe Limited and its group companies. When you purchase a service or set up a Direct Debit you are taken to a payment page hosted by Stripe, where Stripe collects the information it needs to process the payment. That normally includes your name, email address, telephone number, billing address and bank or card details.

The CG Performance website does not receive, process or store your card details or bank details. Those details are held by Stripe under its own privacy policy, which is available at stripe.com/gb/privacy.

CG Performance can see the information Stripe makes available in its dashboard, such as your name, contact details, the service purchased, the payment status and the status of a Direct Debit mandate. This is used to administer your service.

4. Why the information is collected

Personal information is used to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and discuss the service you are interested in
  • Arrange, schedule and deliver coaching sessions
  • Administer purchases, monthly billing and cancellations
  • Keep the records CG Performance is required to keep as a business
  • Communicate with you about your service

The lawful bases relied on are your consent when you submit an enquiry, the performance of a contract once you become a client, and legitimate interests or legal obligation for business and accounting records.

5. How enquiries are handled

Enquiries submitted through the website are delivered by email to the CG Performance business inbox. They are read by Chris Gkoufas. Enquiries are not published, sold or shared for marketing purposes, and you will not be added to a marketing list on the basis of an enquiry.

6. Service administration information

Once you become a client, CG Performance keeps the information needed to deliver your service, such as your contact details, the plan you are on, session records, programme notes and any information you provide during onboarding that is relevant to safe participation. This information is used to coach you and to administer your service.

7. Service providers

The following providers process information on behalf of CG Performance:

  • Vercel hosts the website and processes the technical request data required to serve it, including IP addresses in server logs.
  • Resend delivers enquiry form submissions by email to the CG Performance inbox.
  • Stripe processes payments, Direct Debit mandates and subscriptions.
  • Google provides the email service used for the CG Performance inbox.

Each provider handles personal information under its own terms and privacy policy. Some of these providers operate internationally and may transfer information outside the United Kingdom under the safeguards described in their own documentation.

8. Data retention

Enquiries that do not lead to a service are kept for up to 24 months and are then deleted. Client records are kept for the duration of the coaching relationship and afterwards for as long as required for tax, accounting and legal purposes. Payment records held by Stripe are retained under Stripe's own retention rules.

9. Your rights

Under United Kingdom data protection law you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information is held about you and request a copy
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
  • Ask for your information to be deleted where there is no continuing reason to keep it
  • Ask for processing to be restricted, or object to it, in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Ask for your information in a portable format in certain circumstances

To exercise any of these rights, email chrisgkoufas.performance@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with the response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics cookies, advertising cookies or tracking scripts, and it does not set any cookie of its own. Because there are no non essential cookies, no cookie banner is shown.

If you continue to a Stripe payment page, Stripe sets the cookies it needs to process the payment securely and to prevent fraud. Those cookies are described in Stripe's own policy.

11. Security

The website is served over HTTPS. Payment and bank details are handled entirely by Stripe and never reach the CG Performance website or its servers. Access to the business email inbox and the Stripe dashboard is restricted to Chris Gkoufas.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date at the top.
