CG Performance provides In Person Personal Training, the Movement Strategy Analysis Consultation, Online Coaching and Virtual Coaching. 3. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in pounds sterling.
  • Monthly In Person Personal Training, Online Coaching and monthly Virtual Coaching are recurring services paid by Bacs Direct Debit.
  • The regular monthly billing date is the first of each month.
  • Session packs and the Movement Strategy Analysis Consultation are one time payments.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. CG Performance does not hold your card or bank details.
  • Bacs Direct Debit is not an immediate payment method. A submitted instruction is not confirmation that payment has cleared.

4. When a monthly service starts

  • If you set up a Direct Debit on the first of the month, your first monthly billing cycle may begin immediately.
  • If you set it up on any other day, your first complete monthly billing cycle begins on the next applicable first of the month.
  • No prorated monthly amount is charged automatically before that date.
  • No monthly sessions and no Online Coaching service are included before the first monthly billing cycle begins.
  • Sessions arranged before the monthly billing cycle begins are charged separately at the per session rate of the plan you selected, and are arranged directly with CG Performance.

5. Monthly session allowances

  • The session allowance applies to the relevant calendar month.
  • Unused sessions expire at the end of the calendar month.
  • Unused sessions do not roll over and cannot be credited against a future month.
  • Sessions are arranged by appointment and preferred times are subject to availability.
  • Purchase does not guarantee a specific recurring appointment time.

6. Session packs

  • The 3 Session Pack is valid for one month from the date of purchase.
  • The 10 Session Pack is valid for three months from the date of purchase.
  • Unused pack sessions expire at the end of the stated validity period.
  • Session packs are one time purchases. They are not subscriptions or Direct Debits.

7. Session duration

In Person Personal Training sessions, Virtual Coaching sessions and Movement Strategy Analysis Consultations each last 60 minutes.

8. Cancelling or rescheduling a session

  • A booked session can be cancelled or rescheduled when at least 24 hours' notice is provided.
  • A replacement session is subject to availability and must take place within the same calendar month for a monthly plan, or within the remaining validity period for a session pack.
  • Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours' notice are charged in full, are deducted from the monthly allowance or pack, are not refunded, cannot be rescheduled, cannot be transferred to another calendar month and cannot be credited against a future purchase.
  • A missed session or a no show is treated in the same way.
  • If CG Performance cancels a session, you are not charged for it, the session is rearranged and it does not expire because of that cancellation.

This policy applies to In Person Personal Training, session packs, Movement Strategy Analysis Consultations, Virtual Coaching and scheduled Online Coaching review calls.
