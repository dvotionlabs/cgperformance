01 / THE IDEA

Is 30 minutes enough?

It can be. The goal isn't to squeeze an hour's worth of exercises into half the time. It's to select the work that matters and do it well.

Two regular sessions per week give your training structure. Each session has a clear purpose and forms part of your wider programme.

01 / BEFORE YOU BEGIN

An initial consultation

We discuss your goals, training history, injuries and how you currently move. Your programme begins with you, not a generic template.

02 / EACH SESSION

Purposeful movement preparation

We prepare for the demands of that day's work, then move into three to five exercises selected for your current training phase.

03 / OVER TIME

Progressive strength training

Typically two focused working sets per exercise. We adjust the exercise, resistance and effort as your ability develops.

02 / WHY PERSONAL COACHING

Not just more repetitions. Better decisions.

We pay attention to the way you're moving as well as what you're lifting. The aim is to build strength and physical capacity without unnecessarily restricting your movement options.

  • Individual exercise selection and coaching throughout
  • Appropriate effort rather than chasing failure on every set
  • A programme reviewed and adjusted as you progress

03 / BUILT AROUND YOU

Same session length.
Different programme for every person.

Someone returning to the gym won't need the same exercise selection or training demands as an experienced runner. The plan starts with your goals and abilities, then changes as you do.

05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you start.

Is a 30-minute session enough to build strength?

A 30-minute coached session can support meaningful strength development when exercises, effort and weekly frequency are planned appropriately. Your progress comes from the programme over time, not from a single workout.

What happens in those 30 minutes?

We start with targeted movement preparation and then work through approximately three to five exercises. Sets, repetitions and loads are selected to suit the person and the training phase.

Will I have a training programme written for me?

Yes. We start with an initial consultation to understand your goals and training history. Your programme is individual, reviewed and adjusted as you progress.

Where can I train?

In person at Liverpool Street or Marylebone, London. We'll agree your preferred location and usual training times before you begin.

How do I pay and what happens if I need to reschedule?

The proposed plan is £440 every four weeks for eight sessions, paid by recurring Direct Debit. We confirm your training slots, cancellation and rescheduling terms before arranging payment.
