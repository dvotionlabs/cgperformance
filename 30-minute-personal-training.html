Questions

What you should know.

Is 30 minutes enough to make progress?

Yes, a focused session can contribute meaningfully to your training when exercise selection, intensity and weekly frequency are appropriate. Your programme is designed around the time available, rather than squeezing an hour of exercises into half an hour.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No. We begin with a consultation to understand your goals, experience and needs, then select exercises and training demands accordingly.

What happens during a session?

We start with individual movement preparation before moving into structured training. A typical session includes three to five exercises, with volume and intensity determined by your programme.

How many times per week should I train?

We generally recommend two or three coached sessions per week for this format. Your overall activity, goals and recovery all influence the recommendation.

Where does training take place?

This offer is being developed for Liverpool Street, London. We will confirm the exact training location and available times during your enquiry.

How does payment work?
